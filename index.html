FAQ

Common questions before you inquire.

How does booking work?

You send an inquiry with your dates, guest count, and purpose of stay. If your request is a fit, you receive a private quote, guest agreement, and deposit instructions.

Is this instant booking?

No. RedDoor3 is inquiry-first only. This keeps the experience curated, private, and respectful for both host and guests.

What are the house rules?

No parties, no smoking, no pets, and no disruptive behavior. Quiet hours begin at 10 PM. Guests are expected to treat the property and neighbors with care and respect.

Is parking included?

There is plentiful free street parking nearby. Specific tips can be shared before arrival.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 48 hours before check-in time. After that, any refund is at host discretion unless otherwise stated in your written booking terms.

Can I stay long-term?

RedDoor3 is best suited for short stays and select approved furnished stays. Tell us your dates and purpose, and we will let you know what is possible.
